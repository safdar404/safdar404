# AI-HealthAssist — Clinical Decision Support & Triage System

**Live application:** https://ai-healthassist.ai.studio

AI-HealthAssist is an AI-powered healthcare decision-support and triage application designed to help users organize health information, interpret health-related inputs, identify potential risk signals, and support safer next-step decisions.

## Highlights

- AI-assisted health guidance and conversational interaction
- Clinical-style triage and risk-oriented workflows
- Structured health information and decision-support views
- Safety-focused escalation concepts for higher-risk situations
- Responsive web application experience

## Role

**AI Engineer / Product & Solution Developer** — designed and developed as an applied AI healthcare solution, with emphasis on practical workflows, responsible AI, user experience, and decision support.

> **Important:** AI-HealthAssist is a decision-support/educational tool and is not a substitute for qualified medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice. Users should consult an appropriate healthcare professional for medical decisions.
